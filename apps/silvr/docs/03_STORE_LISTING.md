## English

**Subtitle (30)**: The film camera that keeps your negatives

**Promotional text**
Shoot like film, choose later. Load a roll, make every frame count and wait for the lab. Your original negatives are kept, so you can re-develop any shot with another film stock.

**Description**
SILVR shoots like real film and develops like a real darkroom.

• Roll-based shooting: 10 to 99 shots depending on the camera; count every frame.
• Choose your wait: instant, 1 hour, tomorrow 9 AM or 3 days.
• Darkroom re-develop: negatives are never baked. Change the stock, push or pull ±2 stops, add expiry, grain, halation, light leaks and borders at any time.
• 14 cameras that each shoot differently: half-frame pairs, a 4-lens action camera, double exposures, ejecting instant prints, a disposable whose flash you charge, a 180° fisheye, anamorphic scope, panoramas, a 2003 digicam and a 4-colour pixel camera.
• 14 original film stocks plus 2 digital sensors, from warm Sunny 200 to tungsten Neon 800T and the unpredictable Expired '98.
• Contact sheets: share a whole roll as one sheet of 35mm strips.
• Analog companion: a Sunny 16 exposure calculator and a per-frame shooting log with CSV export for your real film camera.
• Private by design: no account, no servers, no ads, no tracking, no watermark.

**Keywords**: film camera,analog,disposable,vintage,retro,grain,35mm,instant camera,darkroom,light meter,sunny 16
